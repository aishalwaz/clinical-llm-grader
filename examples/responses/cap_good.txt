This presentation is consistent with community-acquired pneumonia. I would first assess severity using CURB-65 (confusion, urea, respiratory rate, blood pressure, age 65 or over). He scores at least 1 for age, so check the other components.

Investigations: request a chest X-ray to confirm consolidation, plus a full blood count, urea and electrolytes and CRP. Take blood cultures if the pneumonia is moderate or severe.

Management: check oxygen saturation and give supplemental oxygen to a target of 94-98%. Start empirical antibiotics according to severity and local guidelines, for example oral amoxicillin for low severity, or amoxicillin plus clarithromycin for moderate severity.

Plan a review at 48 hours and safety net: he should seek urgent help if he becomes more breathless, confused or unable to keep fluids down.
